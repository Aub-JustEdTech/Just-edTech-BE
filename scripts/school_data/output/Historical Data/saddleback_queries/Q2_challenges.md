## Question

Analyze school board agendas, budget presentations, financial reports, superintendent updates, and board discussions from the past 24 months related to district finance, special education, staffing, enrollment, facilities, and state or federal funding. Identify the district's most significant operational and financial challenges, including budget deficits, declining enrollment, special education expenditures, staffing shortages, deferred maintenance, or reductions in state or federal funding. Summarize the challenges by frequency and urgency.

## Answer

The Saddleback Valley Unified School District has faced several significant operational and financial challenges over the past 24 months. Here's a summary of the key issues identified from various board agendas, budget presentations, and discussions:

### 1. Budget Deficits
- The district has been experiencing deficit spending, with expenditures outpacing revenues. The 2025-26 First Interim Budget reflects a deficit of approximately $4.7 million, with projections showing continued deficit spending through 2027-28. Key factors include declining enrollment, rising salaries and benefits, increasing pension contribution rates, health and welfare costs, utilities, insurance, and special education expenses (12.11.25 Minutes, p. 6).
- A projected deficit of $19.7 million was noted, driven by salary agreements, health and welfare costs, deferred maintenance, and rising insurance premiums (12.16.24 Minutes, p. 5).

### 2. Declining Enrollment
- Declining enrollment is a primary financial challenge, reducing revenue growth despite state cost-of-living adjustments. This has led to ongoing structural deficit spending, with expenditures expected to exceed revenues and reserves declining over time if corrective actions are not taken (06.11.26 Minutes, p. 3).
- The Facilities Focus Group has been exploring potential solutions for schools with declining enrollment, including the possibility of repurposing some schools (01.16.25 Minutes, p. 4).

### 3. Special Education Expenditures
- Special education costs have significantly impacted the district’s budget. Strategies to address long-term fiscal challenges include conducting a comprehensive special education review and pursuing alternative funding sources (06.11.26 Minutes, p. 3).

### 4. Staffing Shortages
- The district has been evaluating staffing and program costs as part of its strategy to address fiscal challenges. The annual Reduction in Force (RIF) process is required by the state each year, impacting staffing decisions (05.07.26 Minutes, p. 13).

### 5. Deferred Maintenance
- Deferred maintenance has contributed to the budget's increase, with the district aiming to evaluate program sustainability and manage structural expenses (12.16.24 Minutes, p. 5).

### 6. Reductions in State or Federal Funding
- The district has faced challenges related to the timing of state budget decisions, which creates difficulties for district planning, staffing, and program commitments (06.01.26 Minutes, p. 13).

These challenges are interconnected, with declining enrollment and rising costs being the most frequently mentioned issues. The district is actively working on strategies to mitigate these challenges, including budget reductions, exploring alternative funding sources, and evaluating program sustainability.
