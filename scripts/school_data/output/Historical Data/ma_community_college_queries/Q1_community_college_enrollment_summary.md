## Query 1 — Summary of board discussions

Massachusetts school boards have held substantive discussions on community college enrollment and programs to increase student participation, including dual enrollment, early college, and articulation agreements. Districts such as Wachusett, Whittier Regional Vocational Technical, Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical, Salem, Andover, and others have addressed enrollment trends, program expansion, barriers (such as cost and credit transfer), and board actions to support access. Key themes include expanding partnerships with community colleges, targeting underrepresented and special populations, responding to partner college closures, and approving policies or funding to sustain and grow these initiatives.

### Enrollment levels / trends

- Wachusett reported that Early College enrollment numbers significantly increased over the summer with a total of 183 students enrolled through partnerships with Anna Maria College, Worcester State University, and Mount Wachusett Community College. (Wachusett, 2025-10-03, 10/06/25 Regular #1433 Agenda Approved Minutes 10/03/25 Report 10/06/25 Presentation)
- Wachusett stated that at the time of Anna Maria College's closure, there were approximately 22 planned Early College and dual enrollment course offerings impacting nearly 300 students. (Wachusett, 2026-05-15, 05/19/26 Regular #1444 Agenda Approved Minutes 05/16/26 Report 05/19/26 Presentation)
- Whittier Regional Vocational Technical reported that 128 juniors are enrolled in English Composition I for Fall 2025, with seniors participating in English Composition II and Introduction to Sociology. (Whittier Regional Vocational Technical, 2025-08-06, Packet 8-13-25)
- Revere expanded its higher education partnership with North Shore Community College by launching an Early College Program, which now enrolls close to 150 students. (Revere, 2025-03-30, NEASC Decennial Report)
- Ayer Shirley School District reported that three students are participating in the dual enrollment program, with fifteen students taking and receiving college credit for Intro to Psychology (PSY105) in the fall. (Ayer Shirley School District, 2023-04-04, 15. April 4, 2023 Minutes.pdf)

### Affected student populations

- Wachusett is focusing on recruiting students and families in the 8th grade for Early College, without consideration of prior academic performance, and will prioritize equitable access. (Wachusett, 2024-04-08, 04/08/2024 Regular #1405 Agenda Approved Minutes 04/05/24 Report 04/08/24 Presentation Draft Policy 6619-Life Threatening Allergies)
- Wachusett is participating in the Massachusetts Inclusive Concurrent Enrollment Initiative (MAICE) with Quinsigamond Community College, offering Pre-Q workshops for students with disabilities and transition needs. (Wachusett, 2024-10-07, 10/07/2024 Regular #1414 Agenda Approved Minutes 10/04/24 Report 10/07/24 Presentation)
- Somerset Berkley Regional School District discussed prioritizing traditionally underrepresented students for dual enrollment with UMass Dartmouth. (Somerset Berkley Regional School District, 2025-12-16, 2025-12-16 SB minutes OS.docx.pdf)
- Whittier Regional Vocational Technical's Early College program targets juniors and seniors, providing transportation to Northern Essex Community College for coursework. (Whittier Regional Vocational Technical, 2025-08-06, Packet 8-13-25)

### Programs / partnerships

- Wachusett has partnerships for Early College and dual enrollment with Anna Maria College, Worcester State University, Mount Wachusett Community College, Quinsigamond Community College, Clark University, Nichols College, Assumption University, and Middlesex Community College. (Wachusett, 2025-10-03, 2026-05-15, 2026-06-12, multiple documents)
- Whittier Regional Vocational Technical has an Early College partnership with Northern Essex Community College, with students transported to NECC for coursework. (Whittier Regional Vocational Technical, 2025-08-06, Packet 8-13-25)
- Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical partners with Bristol Community College for dual enrollment and a High School to College Certificate initiative. (Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical, 2024-12-12, 2025-01-09, Policy Sub-Committee, SC Minutes)
- Salem is strengthening the Early College Pathway with North Shore Community College and expanding partnership to Salem State University. (Salem, 2026-06-01, 2026-06-08, Agenda, Minutes)
- Andover reviewed dual enrollment opportunities with area community colleges and Greater Lawrence Technical School. (Andover, 2023-02-02, SC Minutes: 02-02-2023)
- King Philip Regional High School formed a partnership with Middlesex Community College to offer concurrent enrollment of select college courses. (King Philip, 2026-02-09, Draft KPHS Program of Studies 2026-2027 (February 9, 2026 SC))

### Identified barriers

- Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical discussed that dual enrollment is not free for high school students, though some foundation or grant funding is available. (Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical, 2024-12-12, Policy Sub-Committee, SC Minutes)
- Vocational schools do not receive additional state funds to help offset the cost of dual enrollment courses. (Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical, 2024-12-12, SC Minutes)
- Wachusett board members raised questions about how widely college credits earned through early college are accepted at colleges and universities, and about transferability to private colleges. (Wachusett, 2025-10-03, 10/06/25 Regular #1433 Agenda Approved Minutes 10/03/25 Report 10/06/25 Presentation; 2025-09-02, 09/22/25 Regular #1432 Agenda Approved Minutes 09/19/25 Report 09/22/25 Presentation)
- Franklin County Regional Vocational Technical increased the minimum GPA requirement for dual enrollment from 3.0 to 3.6, required placement testing, and maintained a 90% attendance requirement, noting that previous criteria were too lenient and some students struggled in college-level courses. (Franklin County Regional Vocational Technical, 2026-04-08, April 8, 2026 School Committee Minutes)
- Ayer Shirley School District noted that transportation can be a barrier for students who may not have the means to attend courses at Mount Wachusett Community College or Fitchburg State University. (Ayer Shirley School District, 2023-04-04, 15. April 4, 2023 Minutes.pdf)

### Proposed solutions

- Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical accepted a $50,000 grant for the High School to College Certificate initiative with Bristol Community College, allowing students to attend tuition-free. (Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical, 2025-01-09, SC Minutes)
- Wachusett hired a College and Career Specialist dedicated to Early College management after receiving the Early College Planning Grant. (Wachusett, 2024-04-08, 04/08/2024 Regular #1405 Agenda Approved Minutes 04/05/24 Report 04/08/24 Presentation)
- Whittier Regional Vocational Technical added an Early College Advisor to oversee coordination and student support for the Early College program. (Whittier Regional Vocational Technical, 2025-08-06, Packet 8-13-25)
- Wachusett worked closely with multiple institutions, including Quinsigamond Community College, Clark University, Worcester State University, and Nichols College, to identify course offerings and secure faculty approvals after the closure of Anna Maria College. (Wachusett, 2026-06-12, 06/15/26 Regular #1445 Agenda Approved Minutes 06/12/26 Report 06/15/26 Presentation)
- Somerset Berkley Regional School District discussed prioritizing underrepresented students for dual enrollment and using funding from the virtual high school line to support participation. (Somerset Berkley Regional School District, 2025-12-16, 2025-12-16 SB minutes OS.docx.pdf)

### Key meetings

#### 2025-10-03 — Wachusett

- **Topic:** Early College and Dual Enrollment Program Expansion
- **Source:** 10/06/25 Regular #1433 Agenda Approved Minutes 10/03/25 Report 10/06/25 Presentation
- **Findings:** The board discussed a significant increase in Early College enrollment, with 183 students enrolled through partnerships with Anna Maria College, Worcester State University, and Mount Wachusett Community College. Board members raised questions about the acceptance and transferability of college credits earned through the early college program, especially to private colleges, and received clarification on the differences between dual enrollment and early college.
- **Actions:** Board engaged in Q&A, received a presentation, and discussed program expansion and credit transfer issues.

#### 2024-12-12 — Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical

- **Topic:** Dual Enrollment Policy and Funding
- **Source:** Policy Sub-Committee, SC Minutes
- **Findings:** The board discussed whether dual enrollment is free for high school students, noting that it is not, but some foundation or grant funding is available. It was noted that vocational schools do not receive extra state funds for dual enrollment. The only partner is Bristol Community College.
- **Actions:** Approved policy IHCDA – Dual Enrollment; discussed advocacy for additional funding and partnerships.

#### 2025-01-09 — Greater Fall River Regional Vocational Technical

- **Topic:** High School to College Certificate Initiative with Bristol Community College
- **Source:** SC Minutes
- **Findings:** The board accepted the first payment of a $50,000 grant for the High School to College Certificate initiative, which allows students to take courses for credit and attend Bristol Community College tuition-free. The initiative is an articulation agreement and not a dual enrollment program. A guidance counselor serves as liaison between BCC, the district, and students.
- **Actions:** Accepted grant funding; clarified program structure and oversight.

#### 2025-08-06 — Whittier Regional Vocational Technical

- **Topic:** Early College Program with Northern Essex Community College
- **Source:** Packet 8-13-25
- **Findings:** The board reviewed the expansion of the Early College partnership with NECC. 128 juniors are enrolled in English Composition I for Fall 2025, with seniors participating in advanced courses. An Early College Advisor and Aide have been hired to support the program, focusing on coordination, logistics, and ensuring student success. All students are transported to NECC for coursework.
- **Actions:** Oversight of program expansion, hiring of support staff, and transportation logistics.

#### 2026-05-15 — Wachusett

- **Topic:** Transition of Early College Partnerships after Anna Maria College Closure
- **Source:** 05/19/26 Regular #1444 Agenda Approved Minutes 05/16/26 Report 05/19/26 Presentation
- **Findings:** The board discussed the impact of Anna Maria College's closure on nearly 300 students and 22 planned Early College and dual enrollment course offerings. New partnerships were established with Quinsigamond Community College, Worcester State University, Clark University, Assumption University, Nichols College, and Middlesex Community College. The district is awaiting a decision from the Massachusetts Department of Education regarding Early College designation.
- **Actions:** Superintendent reported on new partnerships and ongoing efforts to maintain program continuity.

#### 2023-02-02 — Andover

- **Topic:** Dual Enrollment with Area Community Colleges
- **Source:** SC Minutes: 02-02-2023
- **Findings:** The board reviewed dual enrollment opportunities with area community colleges and Greater Lawrence Technical School. The report included a review of dual enrollment opportunities and noted increased overall district enrollment, but did not specify community college enrollment numbers.
- **Actions:** Reviewed and discussed program offerings and enrollment trends.

#### 2025-12-16 — Somerset Berkley Regional School District

- **Topic:** Dual Enrollment with UMass Dartmouth
- **Source:** 2025-12-16 SB minutes OS.docx.pdf
- **Findings:** The board approved a partnership with UMass Dartmouth to offer dual enrollment courses at a reduced rate of $540 for a three-credit course. Funding will come from the virtual high school line, and there may be an application process if interest is high. The board encouraged prioritizing traditionally underrepresented students.
- **Actions:** Approved program of studies and directed prioritization of underrepresented students.

#### 2026-04-08 — Franklin County Regional Vocational Technical

- **Topic:** Dual Enrollment Criteria Revision
- **Source:** April 8, 2026 School Committee Minutes
- **Findings:** The board approved revised dual enrollment criteria, increasing the minimum GPA requirement from 3.0 to 3.6, requiring placement testing for college-level English and Math, and maintaining a 90% attendance requirement. The changes were made because previous criteria were too lenient and some students struggled in college-level courses.
- **Actions:** Approved revised dual enrollment criteria to better support student success.
